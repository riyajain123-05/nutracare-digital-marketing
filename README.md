NutraCare — Digital Marketing Capstone Project

Revolutionizing Patient Recovery through Personalized Nutrition

NutraCare is a digital healthcare initiative focused on personalized clinical nutrition, patient care, and technology integration.

The project develops an integrated digital marketing strategy for a healthcare nutrition platform that connects medical inputs, dietitian expertise, personalized meal planning, digital engagement, CRM, and automation.

Project Objective

The objective was to design a complete digital marketing ecosystem that moves customers from:

Awareness → Consideration → Conversion → Retention

The strategy focuses on building trust, generating qualified leads, improving conversion, and creating long-term customer relationships.

👥 Target Customers

- In-patients
- Chronic disease patients
- Elderly & long-term care patients
- Caregivers & family members

 Key Business Problem

Patients often receive medical treatment but lack clear and actionable guidance for their daily nutritional needs.

NutraCare addresses this gap by connecting clinical requirements with personalized nutrition and meal delivery.
